# Vexlo

**The AI Audit & Toolbox for Small Business**

Vexlo helps small businesses put AI to work without hiring a consultant or buying yet another subscription. Everything is built around the tools you already pay for: ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini.

🌐 **[vexlo.ca](https://vexlo.ca)**

## What we build

- 🔍 **[Free AI Audit](https://vexlo.ca/audit)** answer a few questions about your business and get a personalized report showing where AI can save you hours every week.
- 🧰 **[Toolbox](https://vexlo.ca/toolbox)** 100+ battle-tested prompts and agent skills for marketing, sales, support, operations, finance, HR, and strategy. Copy, paste, done.
- 🎓 **[Academy](https://vexlo.ca/academy)** free hands-on courses that take you from writing your first prompt to running an AI-operated business.
- ✍️ **[Blog](https://vexlo.ca/blog)** practical guides on automating real small-business work, written for owners, not engineers.

## What's on this GitHub

Here you'll find the open, ready-to-use building blocks behind the Vexlo Toolbox:

- **Agent skills** for Claude and other AI assistants, packaged so you can drop them straight into your own setup
- **Master prompts** that power the workflows on [vexlo.ca](https://vexlo.ca/toolbox)
- Templates and examples for wiring AI into everyday business operations

Star a repo, clone what you need, and make it yours.
